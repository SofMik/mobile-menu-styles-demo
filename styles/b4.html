Style 11 - Sticky Dot Menu

Click the button to switch between mobile and desktop view.

Style 11 — Sticky Dot Menu is a simple and clean mobile menu where the top header stays fixed while you scroll.

This version works just like the regular Dot Menu - tap the ⋮ and a small menu panel opens. The menu closes automatically when you tap outside the panel, tap any link, tap the dot button again, or use the ESC key.

Unlike the regular version, the fixed header and the sticky menu don’t hide when you scroll — they stay visible, and the panel remains open if you’ve already tapped the button. This makes navigation faster and more convenient on long pages. It’s a great choice if you want a clean, minimal look and easy access to the menu at any moment.

You can also use a fixed header with other menu styles, such as Compact Menu, Fullscreen Menu, Slide from Left, Slide from Right, or Slide With Overlay — just mention it in your order.

Price range: 45–65 € depending on integration and animation complexity. The sticky effect may slightly increase the final price if your layout requires additional adjustments for scroll behavior.
