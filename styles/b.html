Style 2 — Fullscreen Menu

Click the button to switch between mobile and desktop view.

Style 2 — Fullscreen Menu is a bold and modern full‑screen navigation. On small screens, the main navigation hides and is replaced by a hamburger button that opens the full‑screen layout. Tap the ☰ button to open the menu.

The menu closes automatically when you tap any link, tap the close button, use the ESC key or swipe down gently — a gesture that feels natural on mobile devices.

Price range: 30–50 € depending on integration and animation complexity.
