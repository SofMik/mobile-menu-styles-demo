Style 7 — Tap Bar Menu

Click the button to switch between mobile and desktop view.

Style 7 — Tap Bar Menu is a practical and fast bottom navigation bar. On small screens, the main menu is hidden, and a semi‑transparent bar with round buttons appears. The bar stays fixed at the bottom — it’s always within reach, doesn’t cover the content, and saves screen space.

It automatically hides when you scroll down, so it doesn’t block the view. It appears again when scrolling stops or when you scroll up.

The buttons are slightly raised, the active one is highlighted, and a soft ripple appears when you tap — these modern effects feel nice and familiar to users. Try tapping to see the feedback. You can use these effects in other menu styles as well. If you like this feature, just let me know in your order.

Price range: 30–50 € for installing the Tap Bar, basic integration, and standard show/hide animations. Additional visual elements — raised buttons, active‑state highlight, ripple effect, custom SVG icons, or adapting the menu to an existing website — can increase the final price.
