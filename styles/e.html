Style 5 - Slide With Overlay

Click the button to switch between mobile and desktop view.

Style 5 — Slide With Overlay follows the same logic as Style 4 — Slide from Right: on smaller screens the main navigation is replaced by a simple hamburger button, and the menu slides in from the right as a clean vertical panel. Tap the ☰ button to open the menu.

This version adds a background overlay that dims the page and helps focus attention on the menu. The soft blur creates a sense of depth and gives the interface a more modern, app‑like feel.

You can also use the overlay effect with other menu styles, such as Slide from Right, Slide from Left, and Popup Menu.

The menu closes automatically when you tap any link, tap the darkened overlay, press the close button, scroll the page, or use the ESC key. It also supports a right‑swipe gesture, which feels natural on mobile devices.

Price range: 40–50 € depending on integration and animation complexity.
