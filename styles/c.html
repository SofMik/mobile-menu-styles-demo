Style 3 - Slide from Left

Click the button to switch between mobile and desktop view.

Style 3 — Slide from Left is a smooth and practical mobile menu that opens from the left side of the screen. On smaller devices, the main navigation hides and is replaced by a hamburger button that opens a clean vertical panel. Tap the ☰ button to open the menu.

The menu closes automatically when you tap outside the panel, tap any link, press the close button, tap the hamburger button again, scroll the page, or use the ESC key. It also supports a natural gesture — you can close it with a left‑swipe, which feels intuitive on mobile devices.

Price range: 30–40 € depending on integration and animation complexity.
