Style 10 - Bubble Menu

Click the button to switch between mobile and desktop view.

Style 10 - Bubble Menu is a light, playful way to show mobile navigation. Instead of one floating button, you get a small group of round “bubbles” that pop out when you tap the main button. They open in a little fan shape, feel friendly and keep the screen clean on small devices.

The menu closes, when you tap outside the FAB, when you tap the FAB again, when you tap any bubble link or use the ESC key. The button reacts to scrolling — if you scroll down, it shrinks into a small “mini” version so it doesn’t get in the way. When you scroll up or tap it, the button goes back to its normal size. This keeps the menu from disappearing by accident and makes it easy to use.

The main button and the bubbles have a clean, slightly raised look that people find very comfortable and modern.

Price range: 50–80 € depending on the number of bubble icons, the opening animation, and how the menu should behave on scroll. Custom SVG icons or adapting the Bubble Menu to an existing website can increase the final price.
