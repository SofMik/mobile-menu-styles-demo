Style 9 — Floating Button Menu

Click the button to switch between mobile and desktop view.

Style 9 — Floating Button Menu is a modern and convenient floating button menu. On small screens, the main navigation is replaced with a floating button in the corner. When you tap it, it opens upward into small round icons. Clean, simple, and comfortable to use on mobile.

The menu closes in all the usual situations — when you tap the FAB button again, when you tap outside the button, when you tap any menu icon, when you scroll, when you press the ESC key, or when you swipe down.

The button reacts to scrolling: when you scroll down, it shrinks into a small “mini” version so it doesn’t get in the way. When you scroll up or tap the button, it returns to its normal size. This keeps the menu easy to reach and prevents accidental hiding.

The main button has smooth animations — it rotates, darkens slightly, and gets a soft highlight when opened. It also has a subtle floating effect: the button gently moves up and down, making the page feel like it’s breathing — just tell it in your order, if you like it.

Price range: 40–70 € depending on integration, button animations, and menu behavior. Custom SVG icons or adapting the menu to an existing website may increase the final price.
