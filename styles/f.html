Style 6 — Popup Navigation

Click the button to switch between mobile and desktop view.

Style 6 — Popup Navigation is a light and modern mobile menu where a compact hamburger button in the top‑right corner opens a small popup panel. This approach saves screen space and works especially well on smaller devices. Tap the ☰ button to open the menu.

The menu closes when you tap the hamburger button again, tap any link, tap outside the popup panel, scroll the page, or use the ESC key.

Price range: 30–50 € depending on integration and animation complexity. Using SVG icons or adapting the menu to an existing website may increase the cost.
