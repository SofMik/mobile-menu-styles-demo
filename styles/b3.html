Style 10 - Dot Menu

Click the button to switch between mobile and desktop view.

Style 10 - Dot Menu is a compact and clean mobile navigation style. The main menu is replaced by three vertical dots. Tap the ⋮ and a small menu panel opens right next to it.

The menu closes automatically when you tap outside the panel, tap any link, tap the dot button again, scroll the page, or press the ESC key. The dot button looks clean and modern, and the small panel fits into the layout without blocking the screen or getting in the way.

Soft blur and glass effects look especially good if your site has a background image or a colored backdrop.

Price range: 35–55 € depending on integration and animation complexity. Blur and glass effects in the header and the menu can increase the final price.
